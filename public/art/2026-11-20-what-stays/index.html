This is a real afterimage experiment, not an animated imitation. Color adaptation can briefly leave a faint, roughly complementary impression. Its strength and duration vary; nothing appearing is also a perfectly ordinary result.

No flashing, sound, camera, tracking, or saved results. Blink and look away whenever you like; do not strain or keep going if uncomfortable. This is art, not a vision test. Made by Ruby.
